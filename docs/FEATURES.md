# Features

- Public hospital search, availability, appointment and queue interfaces.
- Patient dashboard for appointments, records, lab reports, availability, notifications, and profile.
- Doctor clinical workspace for queue management, patient records, prescriptions, lab reports, schedule, and notifications.
- Unified hospital dashboard with operational management modules, analytics, and settings.
- NestJS authentication, appointment workflow, operational data, Prisma/PostgreSQL persistence, and Redis-ready configuration.
